Hubert Boorder & Ilan Rosenberg

With many years in the film industry, Hubert and Ilan Rosenberg have steadily built careers as Stunt Performers, Fight & Stunt Coordinators. In the last 5 years they have broaden there repertoire with producing, directing, writing and acting. Over those years, they've developed a reputation for dedication, collaboration, and a genuine commitment to helping every project — and every person involved — reach its full potential.

Together, they have directed and produced films that have been recognized at festivals around the world, earning over 70 awards. These honors include distinctions such as Best Picture, Best Actor and Actress, Best Stunts, Best Cinematography, Best VFX, Best Comedy, Best Action, and many others — achievements they view as a reflection of the talented teams they've had the privilege to work alongside.
